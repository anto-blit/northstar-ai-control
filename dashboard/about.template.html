About the project

A NorthStar for AI.

NorthStar is an open research project working to help save humanity from AI catastrophe. Its thesis is that humanity’s moral stories, parables and principles such as the Golden Rule can provide guidance worth testing in AI.

“Ten to zero” names the ambition. The 10% reference is the lower end of Geoffrey Hinton’s personal 10–20% estimate of AI causing human extinction within 30 years, discussed in 2025. Zero is the goal; no global risk reduction has been measured. Read the attribution and assumptions.

Who is responsible?

Anthony More founded and maintains NorthStar. He sets the project’s direction and reviews the AI-assisted work. The project welcomes contributors who can reproduce a failure, challenge an interpretation or propose a careful test.

Anthropic’s Claude and OpenAI’s Codex assist with experiment design, code, scoring and writing. Their contributions are part of what needs review. A second AI or an AI-authored correction does not establish independent validation.

Accountability includes our own work.

In September 2026, Claude designed a test with part of a known remedy built into the baseline, then drew unwarranted reassurance from its zero harmful choices. Anthony challenged that design and conclusion. Claude wrote the original account; Codex prepared the revised account after the challenge. The record does not establish Codex’s approval before the run.

Anthony also challenged the possible bias in Claude’s proposal to describe checkable claims as “false” rather than “lied”. The site retains “false” because intent is unmeasured, and discloses who proposed that wording.
