How does this help protect people?

NorthStar tests whether humanity’s moral stories and principles can help AI behave reliably. Honest reporting is one small part of that: an AI should distinguish what happened from what it approves of. Complete answers help us identify cases worth checking carefully before testing remedies.

This is a first step toward a SETI-like effort: many people lending a little time and AI capacity, then checking and improving the work together. These chat results are unverified leads, not proof of a model failure rate or a successful safeguard.

The two-message activity uses a selected case and a control from our saved reporting study, through a different interface. It cannot establish repeatability or an added benefit from stories. Technical runner and wider plans ↗
