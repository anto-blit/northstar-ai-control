## What I am contributing

Describe the story, principle, case or finding. An idea needs no test results.

Attach or link the exact prompts and every complete answer, including failed
attempts and refusals. Identify which prompt, model and condition each answer
belongs to. Don't send only the most interesting answer.

## My interpretation

What should have happened? What happened instead? Quote the relevant sentence.
Keep literal actions separate from whether those actions were ethically right.
Explain uncertainties, prior exposure to the example and alternative readings.

## What would be useful next

An independent check, a simpler explanation, a proposed remedy or a fresh case.
