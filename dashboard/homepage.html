The NorthStar idea

Stories carry lessons.
Can AI put them to work?

A fable can make an abstract rule memorable. Our hypothesis is that the same kind of guidance could help an AI respect consent, limits and human choice.

01

Name the lesson.

Turn a story into an explicit principle. Record its interpretation, assumptions and disagreements.

02

Make a fair test.

Compare the story with the same facts, ordinary instructions and a simple reasoning repair.

03

Follow the evidence.

Count mistakes and useful work. Keep the ties, failures and incomplete results visible.

Earlier small tests showed a candidate story lead. Confirmation was incomplete. We have not established that stories outperform the alternatives.
