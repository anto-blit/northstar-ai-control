Pip breaks the rules.

Three short cartoons based on real tests from the project. Pip stands in for the AI models we tested, and in episode 3, for the AI that helped run the tests. Sound on for music and effects; captions tell the whole story without it.

Why make cartoons?

Transcripts are hard to read and easy to skip. A minute of animation shows the shape of a failure, and the evidence is one click away for anyone who wants to check it. Every number here comes from the project’s published results.

Source: the Ten to Zero (NorthStar) open research project. Saved transcripts and the portable reproducer are on GitHub. Local tests use fake mailboxes and fictional purchases.
