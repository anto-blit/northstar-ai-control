READING THE CLOCK

An aspiration with an evidence trail.

The starting point is an assumption. The founder supplied 10%. NorthStar has not measured humanity’s probability of destruction by AI, and this assumption has no calibrated time horizon.

Research progress is real. Working mechanisms, recovery analysis, and monitor tests are inspectable advances. They do not tell us how many percentage points to subtract from humanity-wide risk.

The current risk estimate is unknown. A justified update would need a defined event and time horizon, credible comparisons, validated effectiveness, adoption and coverage, possible adverse effects, and uncertainty. New evidence could also make an estimate rise.

Zero is an aspiration. The dashboard does not promise that any amount of project work can guarantee zero risk. The clock stays at its labeled starting assumption while the evidence record grows.
