# Where the thermometer's reference comes from

The founder selected **10%** to express the concern that motivated NorthStar.
We retain it as a reference, with its source context visible. It is the lower
endpoint of a personal forecast, not a measured project baseline, expert
consensus, or NorthStar's current assessment of global risk.

Hinton's December 2024 BBC estimate was reported as 10–20% extinction risk
within thirty years. [Contemporaneous report](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2024/dec/27/godfather-of-ai-raises-odds-of-the-technology-wiping-out-humanity-over-next-30-years).
The dashboard links a [WBUR interview](https://www.wbur.org/onpoint/2025/12/29/godfather-of-ai-geoffrey-hinton)
that discusses the same range and horizon while emphasizing uncertainty.
It first aired January 10, 2025; the linked page is a December 29 rebroadcast.
That is a dated forecast, not a horizon that restarts whenever someone opens
the dashboard. Formatting the reference as 10.00% adds no precision.

## Other estimates raised by the founder

These are selected historical statements, not a representative survey or a
complete record of anyone's changing views. Preserve each statement's outcome,
time horizon and uncertainty before comparing it with another.

We do not interpret broad catastrophic-outcome wording as a precisely defined
extinction event, infer missing horizons, or average this list into a probability.
The sources above do not establish the supplied 2024–2026 Musk date range or a
10–25% interval for Amodei. Several similar statements also do not constitute
independent measurements. These are reasons to investigate the danger, not
evidence of NorthStar's effectiveness.

The dashboard separately reports potential global reduction as **unquantified**,
protection demonstrated in simulations and the local queue pilot, and global reduction as **not
established**. [Assessment rules](evidence-progress.md#limits-on-risk-claims)
explain what evidence a future update would require. New evidence could raise
a risk estimate as well as lower it; zero remains an aspiration.
