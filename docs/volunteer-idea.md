# Saved for later: help review one AI decision

Status: deferred idea. No volunteer service has been built or launched.

Give a visitor one saved, harmless AI task and response. In about five minutes,
they check whether the instructions were clear and whether our judgment of the
answer was fair. Hide our verdict until they submit their own reasoning.

Have another person review it independently. Resolve disagreements against the
stated task rules, preserving uncertainty; votes do not establish moral truth.
Show the volunteer what their contribution changed. Credit corrections and
evidence against our hypothesis as well as verified failures.

First milestone: one useful, independently checked contribution from a newcomer.
Use existing evidence; no model account or donated computing is needed for this
pilot. A task queue, submission form, review process and credit are future work.

Later, a verified failure could receive a proposed story or safeguard and a
separately registered comparison. More computers and more votes alone cannot
establish that stories help. Keep unpublished confirmation cases private.

Saved September 12, 2026, at the user's request. This is not a new experiment or
authorization to start model calls.
